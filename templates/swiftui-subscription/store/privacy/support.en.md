# {{APP_NAME}} Support

Need help with {{APP_NAME}}? Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will answer as soon as we can.

## Common questions

- **How do I cancel my subscription?** Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting
  the app does not cancel it.
- **How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?** Open the paywall or Settings and tap "Restore
  Purchases". If you linked Sign in with Apple, sign in first.
- **Why did I reach a limit?** Premium includes fair daily limits; they reset at midnight in your
  time zone.
- **How do I delete my data?** Settings → Delete account removes everything we store immediately.
